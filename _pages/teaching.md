## Students

**Final Year Engineering Internship (6 months)** — Since February 2026

Supervising a 3rd-year engineering student (equiv. M2) on the topic: *Integration of a deep reinforcement learning-based social robot navigation system into ROS Nav2 for transfer to a real robot*, in the context of the SolarNAV project. Main tasks include integrating a learned policy into ROS 2/Nav2, adapting it to the target platform, validating in simulation, and evaluating on a real robot.

## Course Descriptions

**IAR – Artificial Intelligence for Robotics:** Overview of core deep learning algorithms and their applications to robotics, with an introduction to AI research for autonomous systems. Prepared course materials, delivered lectures, and contributed to exam design.

**SY40 – Operating System Architecture:** Essential concepts of modern operating systems, multitasking, shared resources, inter-process communication and synchronization, with hands-on practice under Linux. Led tutorials and labs, and contributed to student evaluation.

**RN40 – Fundamentals of Algorithms and Programming:** Design of iterative and recursive algorithms, data structures, C implementation, and introduction to compilation. Led lab sessions and contributed to evaluation.

**RO50 – Robotics Project and Embedded Systems:** Integrative project combining simulators, perception, embedded computing, networking, and project management. Supervised student teams and evaluated deliverables.

**RO52 – Robotic Algorithms and Real-Time Systems:** Real-time systems, continuous/discrete modeling, classical control techniques, scheduling, synchronization, and distributed/IoT control. Led lab sessions and contributed to grading.

**WE4B – Advanced Web Technologies:** Design of advanced web applications using JavaScript and TypeScript, with frameworks such as Angular and React. Led tutorials and supported students in structuring their applications.
